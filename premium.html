How to get Premium

Three steps

  1. 1

    Join on Ko-fi

    Pick the Lumo Premium membership ($4.99/month) on Ko-fi.

  2. 2

    Claim it in Discord

    In the server that should get Premium, an Administrator runs /premium claim with the email you paid with (or the transaction ID from your Ko-fi receipt).

  3. 3

    That's it

    Premium is on straight away, and every renewal extends the same server by itself. Stuck? Ask in our support server.

Renewals & expiry

No surprises

Renews by itself

Each monthly Ko-fi payment adds a month to the same server, on top of the time you have. Nothing is ever cut short.

Check any time

/premium status shows when the current month ends. Got a code like LUMO-XXXX-XXXX (or an older AETH- one)? /premium redeem still works, and codes stack.

If you cancel

Premium stays until the end of the month you paid for. Then the AI features switch off and your custom name and personality pause until you rejoin. Everything free keeps working. More in the FAQ.
