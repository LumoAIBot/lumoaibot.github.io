5. Usage limits

To keep Lumo available for everyone and its costs under control:

  • Each server has one monthly limit on successful image generations, image edits and video generations (together). Currently each person can make 2 per month in a server, and each server can make 15 per month. Videos are 3 seconds long. The limits reset on the 1st of each month at midnight Eastern Time and are shown in /help.
  • AI replies are limited per person and per server each day. Currently each person gets 20 AI replies per day in a server and each server gets 100 per day, resetting at midnight Eastern Time. Music, games, giveaways, polls and server tools are not limited by these.
  • Each server can have up to 10 giveaways and 10 polls running at once.
  • Each person is rate limited (currently 6 requests per minute).
  • Limits, features and models may change at any time.

Balances and game results may be changed, corrected, reset or removed at any time, for example to fix bugs or undo abuse.

7. Premium

Lumo’s AI features (the AI chat, voice, image and video features) need Premium on the server. Premium is a monthly membership (“Lumo Premium”) that you buy on Ko-fi. After paying, a server Administrator turns it on with /premium claim, using the email you paid with or the Ko-fi transaction ID. Each payment gives that server one month of Premium, added on top of any time it already has, and renewals extend the same server automatically. You can cancel on Ko-fi at any time; Premium then stays on until the end of the month you paid for, and the AI features switch off after that. One-time codes from me still work with /premium redeem and add 30 days each. Ko-fi payment info such as your email is used only to deliver Premium and is stored hashed.

Payments are handled by Ko-fi and its payment providers, under their own terms. Which features are included in Premium may change over time, and the rest of these terms (including usage limits and availability) apply to Premium servers too.
