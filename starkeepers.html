Starkeepers · how to play

How to play Starkeepers

Lumo’s night-sky game, explained picture by picture. Catch stars each night, grow your own sky, and light up your server’s sky together.

01
Step 1

Getting started: it’s on by default

Starkeepers is free for every server, and it starts on. A server admin can turn it off in the web dashboard: open the dashboard, pick your server, open Starkeepers and switch it off. Hunts, the observatory and story bosses have their own switches there.

  1. It’s on by default (admins can turn it off in the dashboard).
  2. Optional: pick a shooting-star channel for falling stars and comet swarms.
  3. Everyone: type /sky watch.
02
Every night

The nightly watch and catching stars

At midnight Eastern time a fresh set of stars appears for you (everyone gets their own). Run /sky watch to see them on the Tonight tab and press a star to catch it.

  • Your first catch each night is free; more catches cost a little stardust.
  • Catching a star you already have makes it brighter instead.
  • The tabs at the bottom (Tonight, Stars, My sky) switch screens. Trips, wishes, the story and the server sky have their own /sky commands.

The Tonight tab of /sky watch with tonight's stars, the streak, tasks and the server goal

03
Battles · Beta

Hunts and battle teams

Unless an admin turns it off, the Dimming drifts across the sky and dulls stars until they turn on anyone who comes near. Pick up to 3 of your stars with /sky team, then run /sky hunt to face a dimmed star.

  • Your team: every star has its own HP, attack, defense and speed. Brighter stars are a little stronger.
  • Win and the star is rekindled: you get a little stardust and a chance it joins your sky (a copy of a star you have makes it brighter).
  • Lose and nothing is lost. Your stars are fine.
  • Cooldown: one hunt every 30 seconds, and hunt stardust has its own nightly cap.

Dimmed stars only come from rarities you’ve unlocked, and they’re matched to your team, so a hunt is a fair fight at any level.

Story bosses (on unless an admin turns them off): every story chapter ends with a boss the Dimming swallowed. Face it with /sky boss; win to rekindle it and open the next chapter. Losing costs nothing, and chapters you already have stay yours.

The moon keeps watch too: each phase calls one family closer (its stars show up more often). A new moon is a Dimming night: hunts are tougher and can bring a rarer star, for 1.5× stardust inside the nightly cap. A full moon makes a rekindled star a little likelier to join you, and a half moon adds a fragment to trips.

Star profiles: /sky star (or the View button on catch, collection, team and battle cards) opens any star’s page: its lore, battle stats, moves and passive, how bright yours is and when you first rekindled it. ◀ ▶ step through its family. A star you haven’t found yet shows only its silhouette and a hint, with a Set wish button.

A story boss fight: your team of up to three stars against the boss

04
What you can find

Rarities and families

There are 100 stars in 8 families (Ember, Frost, Aurora, Tide, Bloom, Veil, Lantern and Hollow), each with its own shape. Every star has a rarity, from Common to Celestial. Rarer stars glow bigger and unlock as you level up.

  • Some stars only show up in certain moon phases.
  • If you go a while without a rare star, a safety net makes sure one shows up.

Every family's star art, from Common to Celestial

05
Grow your stars

Brightness and light

Each star has a brightness from 1 to 5. Raise it with stardust on the Stars tab, by catching the same star again, or with fragments from sky trips. Brighter, rarer stars give more light, and light is what lights constellations.

Stardust comes from playing: chatting, voice, Lumo’s games and Starkeepers itself. It is never sold and is never Lumo Coins. Coins only buy looks.

The Stars tab with stardust, light and your brightest stars

06
Two skies

Your sky vs the server sky

My sky is your own picture: every star you’ve caught, in its family color, with names for the brightest.

The Server sky is shared by everyone. You place your stars into its constellations with /sky server, and the whole server lights them up together.

A player's own night sky picture with labeled stars

07
Together

Constellations, regions and perks

The server sky has regions, each with its own constellations. Fill a constellation’s slots with stars of the right families and reach its light target to light it. The label always says what’s missing, for example “needs 1 more star”.

  • Lit constellations give small server-wide perks, like a little more stardust or slightly better odds.
  • Lighting some constellations opens new regions.
  • Everyone who helped gets that constellation’s badge.

The shared server sky with regions and constellations

08
Keep it going

Streak, tasks and the server goal

Watch every night to grow your streak: every 7th night brings an extra star. Each night you also get 3 small tasks (like “catch 2 stars”) for a bit of stardust, and the whole server shares a goal with a small perk when you reach it.

09
While you’re away

Sky trips and fragments

Run /sky trip to send one of your stars on a 2-hour trip. When it’s back, press Collect for fragments of its family (brighter stars bring more). 10 fragments of one family make one of that family’s stars 1 brighter, once per star a night.

  • The first 3 trips a night also give 1 stardust each.
  • Lumo updates the trip message when it’s back. Turn on Ping me if you want a ping.

The Sky trips screen with a star on its way back and collected fragments

10
Optional · long-term

The observatory

Unless an admin turns it off, /sky observatory opens your own observatory. Spend fragments from trips plus stardust on upgrades, and the observatory levels up as you go.

  • Trip time: trips get 15 minutes shorter per step (down to 1h 15m).
  • Task reroll: swap a nightly task you don’t like.
  • Telescope: a small chance to still get a shooting star you missed.
  • Trip slots: send two stars at once (opens at level 3).

Upgrades only spend. They never pay stardust back and never change rarity odds.

11
A star you want

Wished star

Run /sky wish and pick one star you don’t have yet. Every night it gets a small extra chance to show up in your watch, and that chance grows a little each night it doesn’t (up to 10%). Switching stars starts over.

The Wished star screen with the extra chance for tonight

12
Live in a channel

Shooting stars and comet swarms

A few times a day a shooting star falls in the channel your admin picked. The first 3 people to press Catch it keep it.

Now and then a comet swarm passes for one minute (if an admin turned it on). Everyone presses Help catch; every helper gets stardust and the top helpers a bit more.

A comet swarm message with the Help catch button and top helpers

13
Once a month

The eclipse

Once a month the world’s shadow crosses the moon and turns it red for two nights. The Eclipse Ring region opens, rare and celestial stars show up a little more often, every moon star can appear, and more stars fall.

The eclipse animation as it appears in Discord

14
Your progress

Milestones

Collection milestones (your 10th, 50th and 100th catch, a whole family, your first Celestial and more) give a title you can show on your sky picture, a little stardust, and sometimes a new backdrop. Find them under More → Milestones on the Stars tab.

The Milestones list with titles and progress

15
The lore

The story

The stars were never destroyed, only forgotten, and a sky nobody watches slowly goes dim. Lumo, a little lamp-robot, kept one lantern lit and tells the story in 8 chapters plus an interlude.

  • Chapters open as you and your server play: your first catch, your first lit constellation, new regions, an eclipse and more.
  • Each chapter gives a title; some give a new backdrop.
  • Read them with /sky story.

A story card from Chapter 1, The Little Lamp, with Lumo waving

16
Good to know

Tips

  • Watch every night, even for one catch: it keeps your streak.
  • Check your tasks on the Tonight tab; they’re quick stardust.
  • Place stars where the server needs them: the label tells you what’s missing.
  • Send a star on a trip before you log off.
  • Wish for the star that completes a family.
  • Brighten the stars you placed: their light counts for the whole server.
17
❓ Questions

FAQ

Does Starkeepers cost anything?

No. It’s free for every server. Lumo Coins only buy looks (sky backgrounds); stardust can’t be bought.

Why can’t I see /sky?

An admin may have turned Starkeepers off in the dashboard. Ask them to switch it back on.

When do new stars appear?

Every night at midnight Eastern time (ET).

Can I lose my stars?

No. Stars you catch stay yours. You can choose to delete your own data under More → Forget my stars.

Why does a constellation say “needs 1 more star”?

It lights only when every slot is filled and its light target is reached. The label tells you exactly what’s still missing.

Do comet swarms or shooting stars ping everyone?

No. They only post in the channel your admin picked, and they’re off until an admin turns them on.
