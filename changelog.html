Changelog

What's new

Highlights of what changed, newest first.

October 28, 2026

  • Sticky messages. Keep an important message, like your rules, at the bottom of a channel with /sticky or the new Sticky messages page in the dashboard. Lumo reposts it only once the chat goes quiet, so it never clutters things up.
  • Role buttons and banners for stickies. Add up to 5 role buttons to a sticky, each with its own emoji and a live member count. Tap one to get or remove the role. Stickies can also show a banner image.
  • Fixed: each reminder now keeps its own banner.

October 27, 2026

  • Reminders. /reminder posts a reminder card at a set time in your time zone: daily, on chosen days, every few weeks or just once. Add a banner, an icon and a role ping if you like. You can also set them up on the Reminders page in the dashboard.
  • More people can help run things. Members with Manage Server can now run giveaways, polls, welcome messages, reaction roles and level roles.

October 25, 2026

  • /vote. Vote for Lumo on Top.gg and get 1,000 Lumo Coins, once every 12 hours.

October 20, 2026

  • Starkeepers adventures. Team up against wild stars with /sky hunt and /sky team, battle story bosses with /sky boss, visit /sky observatory and read each star's lore with /sky star.

October 13, 2026

  • AutoMod. Block other servers' invite links, scam links, mass mentions and spam bursts. Every rule is off until you turn it on in the dashboard, and you can add a short timeout. Admins and moderators are never affected.

October 8, 2026

  • Levels. Earn XP by chatting and hanging out in voice. Check your card with /rank and the Levels leaderboard on /top. Level-up messages and level roles can be turned on in the dashboard.
  • Premium codes start with LUMO-. Older codes still work with /premium redeem.
  • Fixed: welcome messages now only post in servers where they were set up.

October 7, 2026

  • Hello, Lumo! The bot has a new name (formerly Aetherion). Same robot, same commands, and all your settings and coins carried over.
  • Premium on Ko-fi. Subscribe on Ko-fi, then run /premium claim to turn on Premium. It renews by itself.
  • Badges. Collect 14 badges and show them off on /profile. A few are secret!
  • Lumo Coins. The play-money currency now has a new name. Balances are unchanged.

October 5, 2026

  • Void bait channel. /setup void creates a trap channel that catches spam bots, with a log, timeout, kick or softban.
  • 12 new robot poses and quicker replies to everyday chat.

October 4, 2026

  • Lumo's robot in replies. The robot mascot now pops up in replies, cheering wins and waving hello.
  • Streaks on /profile. See how many days in a row you've chatted, plus your last 7 days.
  • More options. Pick your date channel's time zone, use an emoji picker for reaction roles, and Premium servers can give Lumo its own banner and bio.
  • Smoother games. Faster game tables and a shiny new Coin Toss.

October 3, 2026

  • Welcome cards. New members get a welcome card with their avatar and member number. Customize it on the Welcome page in the dashboard.
  • Music queue. /play now queues songs, with Skip and Queue buttons and /queue.
  • Light mode for the dashboard, plus extra safety: Lumo never pings @everyone by accident.

October 2, 2026

  • Web dashboard for everyone. Server managers can now set up Lumo at dashboard.lumobot.org, including reaction roles and customization, on desktop or phone.
  • Giveaways and polls. /giveaway and /poll for every server.
  • AI channels. Choose which channels Lumo's AI-powered chat answers in.

October 1, 2026

  • Daily streaks. Claim /coins daily on back-to-back days for a growing bonus.
  • Friendlier dashboard and errors. A redesigned dashboard with a Features page, and clear messages if something goes wrong.

September 30, 2026

  • /game hub. Every Lumo Coin game in one place, with result cards and Play Again buttons.
  • Setup helpers. /setup status shows your server's setup at a glance, and /forgetme lets the server owner erase saved data.

September 27, 2026

  • Lumo Premium. For $4.99/month per server, Premium unlocks AI-powered chat, voice and images, plus making Lumo your own.
  • Music controls. Pause and Stop buttons on /play.

Highlights only. Questions about a change? Ask in our support server.
