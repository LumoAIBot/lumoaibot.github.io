Features

What Lumo does, roughly in order of how much fun it is

Starkeepers, music, games and server tools are free. AI-powered chat comes with Premium.

Panels below are illustrations drawn with HTML and CSS, using the bot's real reply text.

01
Starkeepers Free Beta

A night sky your server fills together

Starkeepers is Lumo’s night-sky game, in beta and actively updated. New stars appear for everyone each night: catch them with /sky watch, brighten them with stardust from chatting and games, and place them into your server’s shared constellations.

  • A watch streak and three small nightly tasks
  • A shared server goal each night with a small perk when you reach it
  • 16 constellations, 100 stars and moon-phase rarities
  • Sky trips: send a star away for fragments that make your stars brighter
  • Wish for a star you want, and earn milestone titles and backdrops
  • Extras: the observatory and hunts with a battle team of your stars (on unless an admin turns them off), plus one-minute comet swarms an admin can turn on
  • On by default; an admin can turn it off in the dashboard

How to play Starkeepers →

A server’s Starkeepers sky with regions and constellations, as Lumo draws it in Discord

02
Hunts Free Beta

Hunt wild stars with your team

Pick your three best stars with /sky team, then battle a wild pack with /sky hunt. Every star has its own stats, special moves and an ultimate.

  • Win to rekindle a wild star, and it may join your sky: 15% common, 12% uncommon, 8% rare, 5% radiant, 3% celestial
  • One hunt every 30 seconds, and up to 25 stardust a night from hunts
  • Solo story bosses with /sky boss: beat each chapter’s boss to open the next
  • Losing costs nothing. Your stars are always fine.

How hunts work →

A real /sky hunt battle from Lumo: a team of three pixel stars beats a wild pack of three

03
Music Free

Music, from SoundCloud

Play SoundCloud in your voice channel with /play. Add more songs while one is playing and they line up in a queue. The now-playing message has buttons anyone in the channel can press.

  • Search a title or paste a SoundCloud link
  • A queue of up to 25 songs, with /queue to view or tidy it
  • Pause/Resume, Skip, Stop and Queue buttons
  • Every server plays on its own

/play now-playing panel with Pause, Skip, Stop and Queue buttons

04
Games & Coins Free

Seven games, one wallet

Run /game for the game hub (or /slots and /connect4 for their extra options): blackjack, slots, coin toss, higher-or-lower, trivia, Connect Four and Texas Hold'em poker, all played with Lumo Coins.

  • New players start with 5,000 coins
  • Claim 2,000 more with /coins daily (no options), plus a growing bonus for claiming on consecutive days
  • Win, loss or push card with your new balance, plus Same bet, Double and Quit buttons

A blackjack hand with Hit, Stand and Double buttons

05
Giveaways & polls Free

Giveaways and polls, with buttons

Run a giveaway with /giveaway start or ask a question with /poll create. Members press a button; Lumo does the rest. No AI and no Premium needed.

  • Giveaways: prize, duration, winners and an optional required role, with fair random draws, end early, reroll and cancel
  • Polls: 2 to 10 options, one changeable vote each (or several), live counts and an optional auto-close
  • Saved, so the buttons keep working and timers keep running after a restart
  • Up to 10 of each at a time, from Discord or the web dashboard (beta)

A giveaway with an Enter button and a poll with two vote buttons and live counts

06
Server tools Free

Roles, logs and a tidy server

Set up the basics in a few commands and let Lumo handle the routine work. Reaction roles and Customize live in the web dashboard (beta).

  • Reaction roles and 12 gradient color roles, from the web dashboard
  • Welcome cards, autorole and a date channel
  • 25 log events and /purge
  • Void bait channel with /setup void. Posts there are deleted and logged
  • AutoMod for invite links, scam links, mass mentions and spam bursts, each rule off until you turn it on

07
AI chat Premium

AI, in your channels

Mention Lumo to chat, send images, or talk in voice. Give it a name and personality that only exist in your server.

  • Chat that understands pictures, with web search
  • Live voice chat
  • Custom name, personality, avatar and profile banner, set in the web dashboard (beta)
  • Choose which channels it answers in, and give a channel its own tone

08
And more

And a pile of smaller things

Understands pictures Premium

Reads images from attachments and replies, so you can ask about what is on screen.

Web search Premium

The AI looks things up live when a question needs fresh information.

Images & video Premium

Generate or edit pictures, and make short videos, right in the channel. Each person can make 2 a month, and each server gets 15 images and videos per month (videos are 3 seconds).

Voice Premium

Join a voice channel and talk to Lumo, and it talks back.

Profiles & ranks Free

/profile cards with your level and 14 badges to collect, /top leaderboards and a /server snapshot.

Welcome cards Free

Every new member gets a welcome card with their avatar and name, and you can change the wording in the dashboard.

Levels Free

Everyone earns XP and levels for chatting and voice time, in every server, with a /rank card and a Levels board on /top. Level-up messages and level roles are opt-in in the dashboard.

Starkeepers Free Beta

Lumo's night-sky game: catch stars each night with /sky watch (5 on a full moon, and some only appear in certain moon phases), brighten them with stardust from chatting and games, and see your own sky with /sky view. Together, place stars into the 16 constellations of your server’s sky (14 across six regions, plus 2 that only open during the Eclipse Ring event) with /sky server to unlock badges and small server perks. Shooting stars fall in a channel an admin picks (the first three to press the button catch them), a monthly eclipse opens the Eclipse Ring, and /top has a Stargazers board. 100 stars to collect, no trading. On by default; an admin can turn it off in the dashboard.

Web dashboard (beta) Free

Server managers log in with Discord to customize the welcome card, set up logs and the join role, manage reaction roles, Levels, Customize, giveaways and polls, and see usage.

AI channels Premium

Admins choose where Lumo's AI chat answers: everywhere, only some channels, or everywhere except some. A channel can have its own tone, or answer only when mentioned.

Fair-use limits Premium

Each person gets 20 AI replies a day and 2 images or videos a month. Each server gets 100 AI replies a day and 15 images and videos a month. Music, games, giveaways and polls never count.
